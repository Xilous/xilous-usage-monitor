What gets in the way

  • The collapsed widget shows no numbers. Three 5 px rings nest inside a 48 px disc, so the only way to read anything is to hover.
  • The device rings take as much room as usage, although they are the secondary reading.
  • 58% / 65% reads like a fraction. Nothing says that 65% is the pace, or whether you are over or under it.
  • The pace tick is a short grey slash across a thin ring, and it disappears against busy wallpapers.
  • Reset times sit in a third column, away from the figure they belong to. The 5h and 7d labels are two close shades of cyan.
  • The ring centres are empty, which is the most legible spot on the widget.
